## Requirements and compatibility

Real-TV control requires a compatible television, a private IPv4 address and a working local network. Firmware, television model, network isolation, app/input state and power settings can affect individual commands or availability. Compatibility, discovery, wake/power-on and uninterrupted operation are not guaranteed. The built-in Demo TV does not prove hardware compatibility.

## Trial

The free **1-day Trial** is a non-consumable App Store product. It begins when its verified purchase completes, provides all remote features for 24 hours, and then stops access. It does not renew and does not automatically charge. Changing the device clock is not an authorized way to extend access.

## Full Remote Unlock

The optional **Full Remote Unlock** is a separate one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. Its entitlement does not expire, although compatibility can change. The App Store displays the localized price before confirmation. Eligible purchases can be restored for the same Apple Account. Apple handles payment, refund and purchase processing under its terms.

## Acceptable use and safety

Use the app only with televisions and networks you are authorized to control. Do not use it to interfere with another person’s device or network. Do not rely on the app for emergency, safety-critical or accessibility-essential control without a working alternative remote.
